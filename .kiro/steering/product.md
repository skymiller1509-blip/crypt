# Product - Crypt

Crypt is a vault that visibly decays, built for Kiroween 2026,
Costume Contest.

## Who it is for

- People who keep a note or a secret for a while and forget the
  deadline. The vault shows the deadline on its face.
- Judges and demo viewers who need the idea in under a minute.
  One look at a rotting vault explains it.

## Problem

A normal vault hides its deadline. You seal something, forget it,
and find out too late that it expired. A date in small print does
not make you act. A vault that rots in front of you does.

## Solution

One flow. Seal a vault with an expiry. Watch it decay through five
stages: Fresh, Dusty, Cracked, Rotting, Ghost. Open it before it
rots, or the secret is gone. The decay is the warning system.

## What Crypt is not

- Not a password manager. It stores no real passwords and makes
  no security claims.
- Not an encrypted vault. Secrets sit as plain text in the browser
  and the page says so honestly.
- Not a big app. One flow, one page, zero dependencies.

## Success

- A viewer gets the idea from the demo alone, with no explanation.
- The Dec build finishes in the Dec 1-4 window with one working flow.
- The submission shows real Kiro use: spec, steering, and hooks in
  the repo, and a write-up drawn from that real work.
