# Crypt - the one flow, step by step

One flow only. Everything else is decoration.

## Step 1: Create a vault

- The page shows one empty crypt and a short form.
- Type a vault name. Example: "Birthday note for Ada".
- Type a FAKE secret. Example: "SAMPLE: the cake is in the oven".
- Pick an expiry: 2 minutes (demo), 1 day, 7 days, or 30 days.
- Tap "Seal the vault".
- The vault appears on screen, Fresh, with its name and expiry date.

## Step 2: Watch it decay

- The page checks the time and shows the right decay stage.
- Fresh, then Dusty, then Cracked, then Rotting, then Ghost.
- The stage changes on its own as time passes. Nothing to press.
- A line under the vault says how much time is left in plain words,
  like "6 days left" or "2 hours left".

## Step 3: Open it in time

- Tap the vault to open it.
- If it is still alive, the lid lifts and the secret is shown.
- The vault is marked Opened. Opening ends the vault.
- If it has rotted, the secret is gone. The screen says
  "Too late. This secret rotted away." The secret is never shown.

## Step 4: Start again

- Tap "Bury a new vault" to make another one.
- Old vaults stay listed below with their final state: Opened or Rotted.

## Demo shortcuts (labelled DEMO ONLY on screen)

- "Make a sample vault" fills in a SAMPLE name and secret with a
  7 day expiry, so the video can start in one tap.
- "Make a 2 minute vault" seals a vault that rots in 2 minutes,
  so the video can show the full decay live.
- "Preview decay" slider moves a pretend clock through a vault's
  life, so the video can show all five stages without waiting.

## What Crypt does NOT do

- No accounts, no login, no server. Everything stays in the browser.
- No real passwords. The page warns on screen: fake demo data only.
- No sharing, no export, no encryption claims. The secret is stored
  as plain text in the browser, and the page says so honestly.
- No extra features. One vault flow, done well.
