# Crypt demo script - under 3 minutes

Record the screen and talk over it. Keep each part short.

## 0:00 - 0:20 - the idea

"Passwords and secrets sit in apps that never show you the deadline.
Crypt is a vault that rots. The worse it looks, the sooner you must
open it. Everything here is fake demo data, nothing real."

## 0:20 - 0:50 - seal a vault

- Tap "Make a sample vault". A SAMPLE vault seals, Fresh stone,
  clean lid, candle burning.
- Read the name and expiry out loud.

## 0:50 - 1:40 - watch it decay

- Tap "Make a 2 minute vault". Wait and watch it go Dusty, then
  Cracked, then Rotting, the eye opening.
- Or drag the "Preview decay" slider through all five stages.
- Say what each stage means as it changes.

## 1:40 - 2:20 - open in time, and too late

- Open the sample vault while it is alive. The lid lifts, the
  SAMPLE secret is shown.
- Let the 2 minute vault rot to Ghost. Tap it. "Too late. This
  secret rotted away." The secret is gone.

## 2:20 - 2:50 - why it is built with Kiro

- Show the repo: the `.kiro` folder, the spec files, the steering
  files, the hook.
- "Kiro wrote this from a spec. Requirements, design, then tasks.
  The spec is in the repo, so you can check the work."

## 2:50 - 3:00 - close

"Crypt. A vault that visibly decays. Open it before it rots."

## Recording notes

- Record while the prototype is running locally or on the live link.
- Keep the fake data warning visible in at least one shot.
- No copyrighted music, no third party logos.
- Upload to YouTube as Public, under 3 minutes.
